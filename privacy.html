Pizza Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Pizza Rush is a game made for children and families. It does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the game stores

Your game progress and zoom setting are saved only on your own device, so the game can pick up where you left off. This information never leaves the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, names, emails or photos.
  • No ads and no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics, tracking, crash reporting or third-party services.
  • No location, camera, microphone or contacts access.
  • No chat and no way to talk to other players.

Children

Because the game collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13. It is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
