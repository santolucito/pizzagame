How to play

Move with WASD / arrow keys, or drag anywhere on the screen to use the joystick. Pinch (or scroll) to zoom.

  1. Walk to the oven to grab fresh pizzas, or stand at the prep table and press Make a pizza to make a family-size one by hand.
  2. Drop them on the counter so customers can take them.
  3. Stand at the register to take the money, then walk over the cash pile to pick it up.
  4. Stand still on a glowing circle to spend money on it: upgrades, helpers, new areas and decorations each have a circle right where they belong.
  5. Grayed-out decorations show where they will go. Buy them to make the shop prettier and busier.
  6. Tables let customers eat in. They leave a tip on the table: walk by to grab it, then clean the table (or hire a cleaner).
  7. Go outside through the door: there are shiny coins in the park and a dog who likes you.
  8. Houses across town order pizza. Carry some out, hop in the delivery car in the parking lot or onto the scooter by the door (big button, or E) and ride to their door. Drive past the drive-thru window to load pizzas straight onto the roof. Deliveries pay double.
  9. The garage across the south road is a car showroom: buy the delivery car and the scooter there (they start white), then drive any vehicle into the bay and tap a colour, rainbow included, as often as you like. Go fishing at the park pond: wait for the bobber to dip, then tap. Hop on the train at the platform for a ride around the top of town.
  10. The old train station north of the farm is boarded up: stand on its circle to repair it, and a train will pull in every few minutes to let off a crowd of hungry passengers. The trash can by the platform path takes any pizzas you want to get rid of. Hop on the train yourself and it carries you through the mountains to Construction Town, where you can ride the excavator, the bulldozer and the dump truck and flatten the dirt piles. The train there waits to bring you home. The bus at the stop north of Main Street takes you to the City (tall buildings, pigeons, and the Pizza Tower with an elevator; stand on a sidewalk by the street and hail a taxi for a free ride around the blocks, then press the button to get out), the ferry at the dock behind River House takes you to the Islands (set up a pizza stand and deliver by jet ski), and there is a secret path somewhere behind the barn. Dusk comes and goes, and sometimes it rains or snows. Past the fire station is an airfield with hangars, a terminal you can walk through, parked planes and a passenger jet: hop in the little plane and it takes off for Cloud Harbor, where you can ride a hot-air balloon, glide between the clouds, and step into the Sky Café for a Cloud Cocoa and a seat by the window. The green helicopter on the helipad is yours too: hop in and fly it anywhere over town, straight over houses, trees and the pond, then press Land to get out wherever you like. Stairs in the office lead upstairs to your bedroom: a nap in the bed starts a fresh morning, the catalog on the bedside table sells toys and furniture for the room (a slide, a drum kit, a fish tank, a race-car bed…), and sitting at the desk opens a drawing pad; hang your picture on the wall and a copy appears downstairs in the dining room. Once you own the ice cream shop you can add an ice cream drive-thru too. The City also has a sports car lot where a super-fast car is for sale; park it on the lot's turntable to pick its colour, and a second one gets delivered to the pizzeria lot (the garage bay paints that one). In the City, the stairs by the subway sign lead down to a station: ride the subway to the Columbia campus, with Barnard across the street, and take it back. Across the south road is a playground: walk onto the slide, a swing, the seesaw or the spring horse to play. The big door on the barn opens too: pat the goat and the pig, chase the hens and pick up their eggs, and climb the ladder to the hayloft window. Fly the helicopter over the pizzeria or the ice cream shop and press Land to hop out on the roof, walk around up there, then hop back in. Buy the telescope from the bedroom catalog and stand beside it at the window to look out over the whole town.
  11. In the City the shops open their doors: the TOYS shop (ball pit, wind-up robot, puppet show), the BARBER (a spinning-chair trim and a lollipop), the BAKERY (ring the bell, croissants, cupcakes and a birthday cake), the ARCADE (claw machine, pinball, dance pad, photo booth), the PET SHOP (puppies, a talking parrot, a bag of treats) the GRAND HOTEL (call the lift, peek under the room-service cloche) the CANDY shop (gumball machine, cotton candy, pick and mix), the BOOKS shop (ride the rolling ladder, read a picture book) the CAT CAFÉ (cats to pat, a feather wand, a kitty on your lap), the GYM (treadmill, trampoline, climbing wall) the FLOWERS shop (water the thirsty plant, grow a seed), the MUSIC shop (drums, piano keys, trumpet, jukebox) the CINEMA (ticket, popcorn and a short pizza movie), the POST office (your own PO box, stamps, postcards) the GROCERY (fill a basket and beep it through the checkout), the SCHOOL (chalkboard doodles, recess bell, story time), the BANK (the big vault, a piggy bank you can always empty again) and the CAFÉ (cocoa with a heart in the foam, a song on the little stage). On the Columbia campus you can walk into Low Library and Barnard Hall through the doors with the blue mats. Stand at a door and press the button to go inside.
  12. Visit the farm past the west road: pick tomatoes, wheat, basil and mushrooms and say hi to the cow for cheese. Farm ingredients make handmade pizzas count for more.
  13. The ice cream shop next door is for sale. Once it is yours it earns money on its own; walk in to collect it.

Your game saves automatically and nothing ever resets. Staff keep earning a little while you are away.
