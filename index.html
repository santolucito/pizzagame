How to play

Move with WASD / arrow keys, or drag anywhere on the screen to use the joystick. Pinch (or scroll) to zoom.

  1. Walk to the oven to grab fresh pizzas, or stand at the prep table and press Make a pizza to make a family-size one by hand.
  2. Drop them on the counter so customers can take them.
  3. Stand at the register to take the money, then walk over the cash pile to pick it up.
  4. Stand still on a glowing circle to spend money on it: upgrades, helpers, new areas and decorations each have a circle right where they belong.
  5. Grayed-out decorations show where they will go. Buy them to make the shop prettier and busier.
  6. Tables let customers eat in. They leave a tip on the table: walk by to grab it, then clean the table (or hire a cleaner).
  7. Go outside through the door: there are shiny coins in the park and a dog who likes you.
  8. Houses across town order pizza. Carry some out, hop in the delivery car in the parking lot or onto the scooter by the door (big button, or E) and ride to their door. Drive past the drive-thru window to load pizzas straight onto the roof. Deliveries pay double.
  9. The garage across the south road is a car showroom: buy the delivery car and the scooter there (they start white), then drive any vehicle into the bay and tap a colour, rainbow included, as often as you like. Go fishing at the park pond: wait for the bobber to dip, then tap. Hop on the train at the platform for a ride around the top of town.
  10. The old train station north of the farm is boarded up: stand on its circle to repair it, and a train will pull in every few minutes to let off a crowd of hungry passengers. The trash can by the platform path takes any pizzas you want to get rid of. Hop on the train yourself and it carries you through the mountains to Construction Town, where you can ride the excavator, the bulldozer and the dump truck and flatten the dirt piles. The train there waits to bring you home. The bus at the stop north of Main Street takes you to the City (tall buildings, taxis, pigeons, and the Pizza Tower with an elevator), the ferry at the dock behind River House takes you to the Islands (set up a pizza stand and deliver by jet ski), and there is a secret path somewhere behind the barn. Dusk comes and goes, and sometimes it rains or snows. Past the fire station is an airfield: taxi the little plane onto the runway and take off for Cloud Harbor, where you can ride a hot-air balloon and glide between the clouds. Stairs in the office lead upstairs to your bedroom, and a nap in the bed starts a fresh morning. Once you own the ice cream shop you can add an ice cream drive-thru too. In the City, the stairs by the subway sign lead down to a station: ride the subway to the Columbia campus, with Barnard across the street, and take it back. Across the south road is a playground: walk onto the slide, a swing, the seesaw or the spring horse to play.
  11. Visit the farm past the west road: pick tomatoes, wheat, basil and mushrooms and say hi to the cow for cheese. Farm ingredients make handmade pizzas count for more.
  12. The ice cream shop next door is for sale. Once it is yours it earns money on its own; walk in to collect it.

Your game saves automatically and nothing ever resets. Staff keep earning a little while you are away.
