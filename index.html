How to play

Move with WASD / arrow keys, or drag anywhere on the screen to use the joystick. Pinch (or scroll) to zoom.

  1. Walk to the oven to grab fresh pizzas (you carry a limited stack).
  2. Drop them on the counter so customers can take them.
  3. Stand at the register to check customers out, or hire a cashier.
  4. Walk over the cash pile to collect your money.
  5. Stand on glowing pads to spend cash and unlock dining tables, a second oven and a drive‑thru.
  6. Visit the Upgrades and Staff desks (or use the buttons) to get faster, carry more and hire help.
  7. Dirty tables need cleaning: stand next to one, or hire a cleaner.
  8. Unlock everything, then franchise to open a bigger, more profitable pizzeria in a new city.

Your game saves automatically. Staff keep earning a little while you are away.
